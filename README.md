## What the projects show

- **Data engineering and ETL:** loading CRM and ERP files into a layered SQL Server warehouse (Project 1)
- **Data cleaning and quality:** SQL checks and rules for duplicates, invalid dates and inconsistent values (Project 1)
- **Exploratory and business analysis:** SQL exploration, reporting views and customer segmentation (Project 2)
- **Data modelling and DAX:** star schemas, time intelligence, relationship control and market share (Projects 3 and 4)
- **Dashboards and communication:** multi-page Power BI dashboards with insights and recommended actions (Projects 2, 3 and 4)

## Learning sources

- **Project 1** follows a data warehousing course by Data With Baraa. The repository is my own implementation.
- **Project 2** builds on the warehouse from Project 1. The SQL exploration and analysis scripts and the customer and product report views follow a course. The Power BI dashboard, the analysis of customers, risks and segments, and the recommendations are my own work.
- **Project 3** is my own analysis of the Northwind sample database.
- **Project 4** uses a dataset and brief from a DataCamp course. The data preparation, model, measures, dashboard and insights are my own implementation.
